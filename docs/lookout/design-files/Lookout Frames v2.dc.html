Nine frames,
one field.

Every frame below is drawn on a 160×48 character grid, in the same glyph vocabulary ratatui can print: half-blocks, shade blocks, box rules, reverse-video bands. Colour is truecolor here and always redundant with a word, so the 256-index tier in theme.rs (29 meadow, 166 bark, 221 butter, 245 ink-3) and NO_COLOR both lose decoration and keep the reading.

Semantic colour
meadow — online, ready, healthy
bark — errored, refused, destructive
butter — watch this, key caps
sky — memory, the one measured blue
ink-3 — headers, hints, unset
Glyph vocabulary
███  fleece pip — three cells of state, beside the word
▁▂▄▆█  sparkline — last ten samples, one cell each
██░░░  gauge — filled against a stated ceiling
▌  selection edge, butter, one cell wide
▀▄  chart body — half-block resolution, 16 steps in 8 rows

03

The editing pane

Butter owns this mode: the title band changes colour the moment you can change something, and every row states when its change lands — now, respawn, next start, read-only. Groups come straight from the group tag on each AppConfig field, and the blurb on the right is the field's own blurb. 1g is what closing it offers.

1e List left, meaning right 160×48

Every field of the group stays visible on the left; the right half explains only the focused one and never moves. Enum choices, the parser's own accepted forms, the default, and the consequence of changing it all live in that panel, so nothing has to be remembered from a manpage.

1i Bleats, full screen 160×48

The feed with nothing above it. Three filter axes stack, and each one is a chip you can drop on its own: which stream (out, err, both), a minimum level, and a text or regex match. They compose with AND — the count beside them says how many lines survive all three — and esc removes the newest chip rather than clearing the lot. The right-hand column is a density gutter for the whole file, one cell per screenful, with a bark tick wherever stderr appears.

What holds across all nine
  • A band names the mode. Meadow means you are looking, butter means you can change something, bark means the link is gone. Nothing else in the frame changes colour wholesale.
  • Every measurement states its ceiling. A gauge without a stated denominator is decoration; each one here prints what it is a fraction of.
  • Blocks carry magnitude, words carry meaning. Remove every glyph and the frame still reads — which is exactly what NO_COLOR and a 16-colour terminal do.
  • Whimsy sits in the chrome, never in the data. Flock, bleats, lambs, afield, whistle. The columns stay plain: pid, rss, exit.

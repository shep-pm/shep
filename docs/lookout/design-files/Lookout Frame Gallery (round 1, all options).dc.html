shep lookout · pane redesign · frame gallery

Twelve frames,
one field.

Every frame below is drawn on a 160×48 character grid, in the same glyph vocabulary ratatui can print: half-blocks, shade blocks, box rules, reverse-video bands. Colour is truecolor here and always redundant with a word, so the 256-index tier in theme.rs (29 meadow, 166 bark, 221 butter, 245 ink-3) and NO_COLOR both lose decoration and keep the reading.

Semantic colour
meadow — online, ready, healthy
bark — errored, refused, destructive
butter — watch this, key caps
sky — memory, the one measured blue
ink-3 — headers, hints, unset
Glyph vocabulary
███  fleece pip — three cells of state, beside the word
▁▂▄▆█  sparkline — last ten samples, one cell each
██░░░  gauge — filled against a stated ceiling
▌  selection edge, butter, one cell wide
▀▄  chart body — half-block resolution, 16 steps in 8 rows
Reading this page

Ids like 1a sit on every frame — name them in chat. Pairs are alternatives for the same pane: 1a/1b landing, 1c/1d detail, 1e/1f edit. The rest are single proposals.

01

The landing pane

Two directions. 1a keeps the table as the spine and spends the extra width on measurement. 1b gives up the wide table for a rail plus a live panel, so the highlighted sheep is already half-open before you press Enter.

1a The meadow table 160×48

The spine survives. Two measurement columns join it — a ten-sample CPU sparkline and a memory gauge drawn against the sheep's own max_memory — and the far right carries the latest bleat, so the feed at the bottom becomes confirmation rather than the only place output lives. The title band is reverse-video meadow: one glance tells you the shepherd is answering.

1b The rail and the pasture 160×48

The flock moves into a 46-column rail and the remaining width becomes the selected sheep's own pasture: history charts, vitals, bleats. Enter then promotes this panel to full width rather than opening a different screen, so j/k reads like scrubbing through the flock. The cost is honest: PID, EXIT and FOLD leave the always-visible list.

The sheep pane

Enter from the flock. Both directions carry the same five things — two histories, process facts, config, env, and a filterable feed. They disagree about which one gets the width.

1c Two histories, two ledgers 160×48

CPU and memory sit side by side at the top, each with its own ceiling stated in words. Below them config and env split the width, grouped exactly as AppConfig groups them, with pending edits marked butter. The feed keeps the bottom third and its filter is visible whether or not it is set.

The editing pane

Butter owns this mode: the title band changes colour the moment you can change something, and every row states when its change lands — now, respawn, next start, read-only. Groups come straight from the group tag on each AppConfig field, and the blurb shown on the right is the field's own blurb.

1e List left, meaning right 160×48

Every field of the group stays visible on the left; the right half explains only the focused one and never moves. Enum choices, the parser's own accepted forms, the default, and the consequence of changing it all live in that panel, so nothing has to be remembered from a manpage.
